# Copywriting + Conversion Frameworks for Local Service Pages

Research territory 03 of the SEO-CONTENT-OS expansion. Author pass: 2026-07-20 PKT. All URLs fetched or searched live this session; where a figure is folklore (single uncontrolled test repeated across blogs) it is flagged as such, not sold as proof. No em dash. Law 8 binding: every recommendation below optimizes a real conversion or trust lever, never detector evasion.

---

## 0. What already exists (so we do not rebuild it)

The system is not starting from zero on conversion. A read of the six playbooks shows the named frameworks are already applied, but **inline and scattered**, re-explained slightly differently in each playbook:

- `service-page.md`: PAS/PASTOR spine, Copyhackers belief-sequencing, Copyhackers 5-element hero, Rule of One, RMBC two-part mechanism, Bencivenga distributed proof, Cialdini (Authority/Social Proof/Liking/Unity, Reciprocity, Commitment), Hormozi value stack, price anchoring, risk reversal at the ask.
- `homepage.md`: StoryBrand grunt test + Guide + Plan, Schwartz stages of awareness, Oli Gardner attention ratio (Conversion-Centered Design), NN/g credibility + information scent, PAS empathy band, voice-of-customer mining.
- Quality gates G0-G12 cover intent, specificity, E-E-A-T, doorway, extractability, keyword, meta, links, readability, voice, sources, schema, compliance.

**The two structural gaps this research targets:**

1. **No canonical frameworks library.** Every framework lives as prose inside a playbook. There is no `knowledge/frameworks/` a writer, the `/brief` step, or the AIOS `plan-post` skill can load as a single source of truth. This causes drift (PAS is defined three ways), bloat (each playbook re-teaches the same model), and no reuse across page types.
2. **No conversion gate.** G0-G12 can all pass on a page that still converts poorly: weak CTA verb, no risk reversal placed at the ask, proof pooled in one wall instead of next to the claim it supports, no price signal, a second co-equal CTA splitting intent. The system's stated job is "convert the local buyer (call/book), not just rank." Nothing in the gate stack tests that directly.

Everything below is scoped to closing those two gaps without duplicating what the playbooks already do well.

## 1. What today's top marketers actually publish (evidence vs folklore)

### Copyhackers / Joanna Wiebe - conversion copywriting as a discipline
Wiebe's core, durable claim: conversion copy "moves the reader to yes using voice-of-customer data, frameworks, formulas and proven persuasion techniques" ([Copyhackers, conversion copywriting defined](https://copyhackers.com/conversion-copywriting-defined/), fetched 2026-07-20). Two load-bearing ideas worth encoding:
- **Voice-of-customer (VoC) mining is the input, not the framework.** The formula (PAS, AIDA) is the container; the buyer's own words are what fill it. Copyhackers' formula guide catalogs 200+ patterns but insists the words come from reviews and calls, not a swipe file ([Copyhackers, copywriting formulas](https://copyhackers.com/2015/10/copywriting-formula/)).
- **PAS is the default when there is a real pain; AIDA when there is not** ([Copyhackers, PAS practice](https://copyhackers.com/the-practice-headlines-and-pas-formula/)). This maps cleanly to local: emergency trades (burst pipe, AC dead in July, injury) are high-pain -> PAS; considered/elective work (kitchen remodel, cosmetic dentistry, estate planning) is lower-pain, desire-led -> AIDA or StoryBrand.

Evidence grade: framework-level craft consensus, not controlled study. Treat as structural.

### CXL / Peep Laja - evidence-based CRO
CXL's discipline is macro-to-micro: value proposition first, then message hierarchy, then micro-copy (CTA wording, form friction) ([CXL, effective website copywriting](https://cxl.com/blog/quick-course-on-effective-website-copywriting/); [CXL copywriting hub](https://cxl.com/copywriting/)). The transferable rule for this system: **do not optimize the CTA button before the value proposition and proof are right.** A page that fails the value-prop test cannot be rescued by button color. CXL is also the best source for treating persuasion as testable rather than dogma; the honest posture the system already takes (framework claims are priors, not promises) matches CXL's stance.

### Nielsen Norman Group - how people actually read local pages
NN/g's eye-tracking corpus (stable for 20+ years): people scan, they do not read; the F-pattern is the default scan of text-heavy content, and content on the right and lower-left is routinely missed ([NN/g, F-pattern](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/f-pattern-reading-digital-content/)). Users read roughly a quarter of the words on a page; the average visit is under a minute. Implications for local pages, most of which are read on a phone under stress:
- Front-load the answer and the CTA (the system's BLUF/inverted-pyramid rule already does this).
- Build a **scan layer**: bolded keyphrases, question-headed H2/H3, short paragraphs, the CTA and phone reachable without hunting. The bold layer must carry the argument on its own for the 75% who never read the body.
- The F-pattern degrades into a spotted/layer-cake pattern when the page is well-structured with headings; good subheads are what break the F. This is a formatting lever the system half-covers (readability gate) but does not specify as a conversion tool.

Evidence grade: strong, empirical, primary-source. This is the most evidence-backed territory of the four.

### Neil Patel / Backlinko (Brian Dean) - on-page + conversion
This tier's material is aggregation-grade, not primary research: useful for the consensus checklist (descriptive CTAs, above-the-fold value, benefit-led headings, trust signals near the ask) but every quantified lift figure they publish traces back to a handful of much-cited single tests. Use for the checklist, not for the numbers.

## 2. The classic frameworks, specified for local service conversion

Each of these is worth a **one-page canonical spec** in `knowledge/frameworks/`. For each: what it is, the local adaptation (emergency vs considered), the PASS test, the anti-pattern. This section is the source content for those files.

### 2.1 PAS / PASTOR (Problem - Agitate - Solve; extended: Problem - Amplify - Story - Testimony - Offer - Response)
- **Use for:** high-pain local intent (emergency trades, injury, urgent repair). The buyer already feels the problem; naming it in their words earns the right to pitch.
- **Local adaptation:** Agitate with the concrete operational and financial cost (the leak that becomes a deck replacement, the small cavity that becomes a root canal), substantiable, never manufactured fear. Amplify locally where a local condition applies (hail season, hard water, clay-soil foundations).
- **PASS test:** the problem is stated in customer-verbatim language (from VoC), agitation cites a real cost, and the solution names a mechanism, not just a service.
- **Anti-pattern:** agitation in the company's words ("your roof may be compromised") instead of the buyer's ("you keep finding little rocks in the gutter").

### 2.2 AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action) and the 4 Ps (Promise - Picture - Proof - Push)
- **Use for:** lower-pain, desire-led or considered purchases (remodels, elective/cosmetic, planning services) where there is no acute pain to agitate ([Copyhackers PAS vs AIDA](https://copyhackers.com/the-practice-headlines-and-pas-formula/)).
- **Local adaptation:** Desire is the picturable outcome ("a kitchen you actually want to cook in," "a roof that lasts 30 years"). Proof is the local, verifiable specifics the system already mandates.
- **4 Ps** is the tighter operational variant: Promise the outcome, Picture it vividly, Prove it with specifics, Push to one action. Good default skeleton for a service-city money page.

### 2.3 StoryBrand SB7 (Miller) - Character, Problem, Guide, Plan, Call to Action, Failure, Success
- **Source:** Building a StoryBrand; 2.0 edition released 7 Jan 2025 with revised exercises ([StoryBrand SB7 overview](https://umbrex.com/resources/frameworks/marketing-frameworks/storybrand-sb7-framework/); [local business examples](https://rebelfishlocal.com/storybrand-website-examples/)).
- **Use for:** homepage and about page narrative order (the system already uses the grunt test and Guide/Plan). SB7's real contribution to local is discipline: **the customer is the hero, the business is the guide.** A service is invisible, so making the Plan explicit ("1. Call. 2. We arrive on time with upfront pricing. 3. We fix it, guaranteed.") lowers perceived risk.
- **Local adaptation:** name the three problem layers - external (broken water heater), internal (stressed, worried about being overcharged), philosophical (a homeowner should not be taken advantage of). The internal problem is where VoC language and Unity live.
- **PASS test:** the page passes the grunt test in five seconds (what, for whom, next action) AND the business never positions itself as the hero.
- **Evidence grade:** craft framework, high explanatory power, no controlled-study proof. Structural.

### 2.4 Cialdini's 7 principles of persuasion
Reciprocity, Commitment & Consistency, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity, Unity. Unity (shared identity) was added in 2016 as the seventh ([Cialdini 7 principles](https://www.cognitigence.com/blog/cialdini-7-principles-of-persuasion); [CXL on Unity](https://cxl.com/blog/cialdini-unity/); [CXL on all 7 for conversion](https://cxl.com/blog/cialdinis-principles-persuasion/)). Local-service mapping (this is the single most reusable framework across all six page types):

| Principle | Local-service application | Where on the page |
|---|---|---|
| Reciprocity | Free inspection / audit / estimate as a genuine gift that obligates | CTA offer |
| Commitment & Consistency | Low-friction first yes ("check my availability") before the full ask | CTA sequencing |
| Social Proof | Real reviews with counts, named + local, linked to third-party profile | Distributed next to claims |
| Authority | License number, credential, named operator, verifiable issuer | Proof + about |
| Liking / Unity | Local rootedness, shared-identity language ("your neighbors in [town]") | Empathy band, about |
| Scarcity | Genuine capacity/seasonal limits only; never fabricated countdowns | Sparingly, at CTA |
| Unity | "We answer in [town]," family-owned, in-group local language | Voice + empathy |

- **Hard line:** Scarcity and urgency must be literally true. Fabricated "3 slots left," fake countdowns, and false stock indicators are FTC-defined dark patterns ([FTC dark patterns / urgency](https://pandectes.io/blog/dark-patterns-in-2026-what-the-ftcs-new-rules-mean/)). Encode this as a rule, not a suggestion. It intersects the existing compliance spine and the FTC review rule.

### 2.5 Schwartz - 5 stages of awareness x 5 stages of market sophistication
From Breakthrough Advertising ([5 stages of awareness](https://betweenthelinescopy.com/blog/stages-of-awareness/); [Schwartz pyramid](https://b-plannow.com/en/the-schwartz-pyramid-guide-to-the-5-levels-of-customer-awareness/)). Awareness: Unaware -> Problem-Aware -> Solution-Aware -> Product-Aware -> Most-Aware. The directness of the copy must match the buyer's awareness.
- **This is a brief-time selector, not a page section.** The target query reveals awareness: "emergency plumber near me" is Solution/Product-Aware (be direct, lead with the ask and proof); "why is my water bill so high" is Problem-Aware (educate first, then pitch); a brand search is Most-Aware (just make the ask frictionless).
- **Market sophistication** (how many competitors have made the same claim) tells you whether "24/7 emergency service" is a differentiator or table stakes in this market. In a saturated local market you must lead with a **mechanism or a specific** ("the flashing is why your last repair failed"), not a claim everyone makes. This is exactly the system's first-hand-specificity gate, restated as a positioning decision.
- **PASS test:** the copy's directness matches the query's awareness stage, and the lead claim is not one every competitor in the SERP already makes.

### 2.6 Copyhackers 5-element hero, Rule of One, belief sequencing
Already used in `service-page.md`. Worth promoting to a framework file because all six page types use a hero: one reader, one dominant idea, one action; the hero must state outcome + who + one trust signal + one action in five seconds. Belief sequencing = each block earns the belief the next block requires before it asks for more.

### 2.7 Value equation / value stack (Hormozi) and risk reversal
- **Value equation:** perceived value = (dream outcome x perceived likelihood of achievement) / (time delay x effort & sacrifice). For local services the two dials the copy actually moves are **perceived likelihood** (proof, guarantee, credential) and **effort/sacrifice** (frictionless CTA, "we handle the permit, the haul-away, the cleanup"). Encode as a diagnostic: which of the four is weakest on this draft?
- **Risk reversal** is the highest-leverage under-used local lever. A guarantee that *costs the business money when it fails* (Benjamin Franklin Plumbing's "$5/minute if we're late") is a credibility signal a hollow "satisfaction guaranteed" badge can never be (from `service-page.md` teardown). Encode the catalog: workmanship warranty with years, manufacturer warranty, satisfaction/refund, "no mess left behind," free re-inspection, no-obligation quote, punitive on-time guarantee.
- **Evidence note:** the widely cited "guarantees lift conversion" claims are mostly vendor case studies, not controlled trials; the mechanism (shifting perceived risk from buyer to seller) is sound and needs no fabricated coefficient. Do not quote a percentage lift to a client.

## 3. Trust, objection-handling, and proof placement

- **Proof next to the claim, not pooled in a wall.** Bencivenga's distributed proof: a testimonial about on-time arrival belongs next to the on-time promise, not in a testimonials carousel at the bottom. NN/g credibility research: external, third-party-verifiable proof (linked Google/BBB/Angi profile) outweighs self-hosted anonymous quotes, which have lost trust weight in the deepfake era ([homepage.md, NN/g]).
- **Objection-handling = friction-and-anxiety reduction.** Every unanswered objection is a silent exit. Order the FAQ the way a buyer raises objections: price -> "will this work for my situation" -> timeline -> warranty -> what happens after I book -> licensing/insurance. Each answer passes the paste-alone test (self-contained, also an AI-citation surface).
- **Urgency without dark patterns.** Real urgency (a genuine seasonal deadline, real same-day capacity) is legitimate; fabricated urgency is an FTC violation and a trust killer ([FTC dark patterns 2026](https://pandectes.io/blog/dark-patterns-in-2026-what-the-ftcs-new-rules-mean/)). The safe local urgency is the cost-of-inaction frame (the problem gets more expensive the longer you wait), substantiated.

## 4. Readability and formatting that lifts local mobile conversion

Grounded in NN/g (Section 1) plus CTA micro-copy data. Flagging evidence grade on each:

- **Scan layer (strong evidence).** Bold the load-bearing phrases; question-headed subheads; paragraphs of 1-3 sentences; the argument must survive being read at the bold-and-heading level only. NN/g F-pattern + 25%-of-words-read.
- **First-person, specific CTA verb (medium evidence, one famous test).** "Get my free roof inspection" over "Submit"/"Get a free quote." The oft-cited "first-person CTA lifts clicks 90%" figure traces to a single Michael Aagaard/Unbounce test ([CTA statistics roundup](https://wisernotify.com/blog/call-to-action-stats/)); the direction is well-replicated, the magnitude is folklore. Use the tactic, not the number.
- **Click-to-call is non-negotiable on mobile (strong, directional).** A real `tel:` link and a thumb-reachable sticky call button; buttons outperform text links substantially on mobile, and emergency-intent local traffic converts by phone ([home services CTA stats](https://cubecreative.design/blog/small-business-marketing/top-10-cta-stats-home-services)). Tap target >= 44px (usability floor above the WCAG 2.5.8 24px minimum, per `homepage.md`).
- **Message match (strong, principled).** The CTA and H1 language should mirror the search query ("emergency plumbing" for "emergency plumber near me"). Mismatch between ad/query intent and page language is a well-established conversion leak.
- **One attention ratio (Oli Gardner, structural).** On a conversion page, clickable things should approach the number of goals. Two co-equal primary CTAs split intent; demote everything but the one action to a text link.

## 5. Recommendations mapped to categories

Legend: effort S (< 1 day), M (1-3 days), L (multi-day). Each names what, why, source.

### [MD knowledge files] / [frameworks] - the biggest win
**R1. Build `knowledge/frameworks/` as a canonical library (one file per framework).** Extract the models now duplicated across playbooks into single-source specs, each formatted identically: *what it is / when to use for local (emergency vs considered) / local adaptation / PASS test / anti-pattern / evidence grade*. Playbooks then reference the framework file instead of re-teaching it.
- Files to create: `pas-pastor.md`, `aida-4ps.md`, `storybrand-sb7.md`, `cialdini-7.md`, `awareness-sophistication.md` (Schwartz), `hero-rule-of-one.md` (Copyhackers), `value-equation-risk-reversal.md`, `objection-handling.md`, `message-match-scan-layer.md` (NN/g formatting).
- Why: kills drift and bloat, gives `/brief`, `/write-*`, and the AIOS `plan-post` skill one place to pull from, and makes the "which framework for this page" decision explicit instead of buried. Source: Sections 2-4 above. Effort: **M** (content is 80% harvestable from existing playbooks + this file).

**R2. `knowledge/frameworks/framework-selector.md` - the routing table.** A one-page decision matrix: page type x query awareness x pain level -> recommended primary framework + hero style. E.g. "service-city page, emergency intent, high pain -> PAS spine + direct trust-led hero + Reciprocity offer." Why: turns framework choice into a brief-step decision, not a writer's guess. Source: Schwartz awareness x Copyhackers PAS-vs-AIDA. Effort: **S**.

**R3. `knowledge/frameworks/voice-of-customer-mining.md`.** VoC is referenced in every playbook as the input to PAS/empathy/FAQ but is never specified as a method. Write the method: where to pull (client reviews, the SME interview, recorded sales calls, Google review mining, "People Also Ask"), how to extract the exact problem/anxiety phrasing, how to route it into hero/empathy/FAQ. Why: the durable moat in an AI-flooded web is the buyer's real words; right now the system assumes the writer knows how to mine them. Source: Copyhackers. Effort: **S**.

### [agents] + [laws/gates] - close the conversion gap
**R4. Add a conversion gate (G13) and a `conversion-optimizer` reviewer.** *This is the second-biggest win and the answer to "is a conversion agent warranted": yes.* The current stack has no gate that fails a page for weak conversion. Add **G13 - Conversion readiness** as a warning-to-fail gate that checks, per page type: single dominant CTA repeated (no co-equal competitor); first-person outcome CTA verb; NAP-consistent click-to-call present; a real price/range signal (not bare "contact us"); a named risk-reversal/guarantee placed at the ask; proof distributed next to claims; framework spine present and matched to intent; scan layer present. Implement as a judgment gate run by a `conversion-optimizer` agent (mirrors how `critical-editor` and `compliance-auditor` already split labor), fed by a deterministic linter (R5). Why: the system's stated purpose is conversion, and nothing currently enforces it. Source: Sections 2-4. Effort: **M**.
- Alternative if a new agent is too heavy: fold the G13 judgment pass into the existing `critical-editor` agent and add the gate row to `gates.md`. Lower effort (**S-M**), slightly less separation of concerns.

**R5. `scripts/conversion_linter.py` - deterministic pre-check feeding G13.** Offline, no API, same pattern as `readability_scorer.py`/`compliance_lint.py`. Flags: count of primary CTAs and whether >1 competing action; presence of a `tel:` / click-to-call token; first-person CTA verb heuristic; presence of a price/range token vs bare "contact us for pricing"; presence of a guarantee/warranty keyword; whether a CTA appears after the proof block and after the FAQ. Returns a structured report the `conversion-optimizer` agent reasons over (deterministic flags, human-judgment verdict). Why: makes the conversion gate cheap and repeatable, and keeps judgment for what actually needs it. Source: Section 4 + existing script pattern. Effort: **M**.

### [MD knowledge files] - formatting + readability
**R6. Extend the readability layer with a conversion-formatting spec.** Either a `knowledge/voice/scan-layer.md` or a section in `message-match-scan-layer.md` (R1): F-pattern-aware layout, the bold scan layer that must carry the argument alone, front-loading, CTA reachability, tap-target size. Optionally extend `readability_scorer.py` to flag paragraphs over ~4 sentences and sections with no bolded scan anchor. Why: the readability gate (G8) currently scores prose difficulty but not scannability, which is the actual mobile-conversion lever NN/g measures. Source: NN/g F-pattern. Effort: **S** (MD) / **M** (if extending the script).

### [laws] - one hard rule
**R7. Encode the "no fabricated urgency/scarcity" rule explicitly** in the compliance spine and the Cialdini framework file, tied to the FTC dark-patterns guidance. It is implied by the existing FTC review-rule coverage but scarcity/urgency dark patterns are a distinct FTC category and deserve their own named line so a writer reaching for urgency knows the boundary. Source: [FTC dark patterns 2026](https://pandectes.io/blog/dark-patterns-in-2026-what-the-ftcs-new-rules-mean/). Effort: **S**.

### What NOT to add
- No new "persuasion score" numeric gate. Persuasion is not reliably quantifiable offline; a fake score invites gaming and contradicts the system's honest posture. G13 stays judgment-plus-deterministic-flags, never a single magic number.
- No detector-evasion or "humanizer" step (Law 8). Not in scope, refused on sight.
- Do not duplicate framework prose back into the playbooks after R1; playbooks should *reference* the framework files, or the drift returns.

## 6. Priority order

1. **R1** frameworks library (M) - unlocks reuse, kills drift, feeds everything else.
2. **R4** conversion gate G13 + `conversion-optimizer` (M) - closes the one functional gap between "ranks" and "converts."
3. **R5** `conversion_linter.py` (M) - makes R4 cheap and deterministic.
4. **R2** framework-selector routing table (S) + **R3** VoC mining method (S) - highest value-per-hour.
5. **R6** scan-layer formatting spec (S/M) + **R7** urgency hard line (S) - fast finishing touches.
