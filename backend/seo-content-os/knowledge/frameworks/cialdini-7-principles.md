# Cialdini's 7 Principles of Persuasion

Canonical spec. Defined once here; playbooks reference this file. This is the single most reusable framework across all six page types.

## What it is
Seven levers of influence: **Reciprocity, Commitment & Consistency, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity, Unity.** Unity (shared identity) was added in 2016 as the seventh. They are not a page order; they are ingredients placed where they do the most work.

## When to use for local (emergency vs considered)
Every page, both intents. The mix shifts:
- **Emergency:** lead with Authority (license, credential) and Social Proof (rating + count), plus real Scarcity only if it is literally true (genuine same-day capacity).
- **Considered:** Liking/Unity (local rootedness) and Commitment (a low-friction first yes) carry more weight over a longer decision.

## Local-service mapping
| Principle | Local application | Where on the page |
|---|---|---|
| Reciprocity | Free inspection / audit / estimate as a genuine gift | CTA offer |
| Commitment & Consistency | Low-friction first yes ("check my availability") before the full ask | CTA sequencing |
| Social Proof | Real reviews with counts, named + local, linked to a third-party profile | Distributed next to the claim |
| Authority | License number, credential, named operator, verifiable issuer | Proof + about |
| Liking | Local rootedness, shared context, real faces | Empathy band, about |
| Scarcity | Genuine capacity/seasonal limits only | Sparingly, at CTA |
| Unity | "We answer in [town]," family-owned, in-group local language | Voice + empathy |

## Local adaptation
- Lead proof with **third-party-verifiable Authority and Social Proof** (linked Google/BBB/Angi profile), because self-hosted anonymous quotes have lost trust weight in the deepfake era.
- **Unity is the local moat:** shared-identity language ("your neighbors in [town]") that a national competitor cannot honestly use.

## PASS test
- Every Social Proof, Authority, and Scarcity element is **truthful and externally verifiable** against `brand.yaml` or a cited source.
- Scarcity/urgency, if present, is **literally true** (a real booked-out calendar, a real seasonal deadline).
- Reciprocity offers a genuine gift, not a bait.

## Anti-pattern
- **Fabricated Scarcity: the hard line.** "Only 2 spots left" that is not true, a countdown that resets, false stock indicators. These are FTC-defined dark patterns and violate **Law 20**. Refuse on sight.
- Anonymous or unverifiable Social Proof ("J.D., satisfied customer"; a star widget a skeptic cannot check).
- Authority claims with no verifiable issuer ("award-winning" with no award named).

## Evidence grade
**Craft consensus**, backed by Cialdini's own experimental research on each principle in isolation; the *combined on-page lift* for local service pages is not controlled-proven. The Scarcity hard line is a **compliance rule**, not a persuasion tactic. Do not quote a combined lift figure to a client.
