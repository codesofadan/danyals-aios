## What "climate controlled" actually means here

Here it means two things you can check: the units hold 55 to 80 degrees, and dehumidifiers pull the moisture down. That is real climate control, not just a cooled hallway. "Climate controlled" is not a regulated term. It can mean anything from an air vent to full humidity control, so we spell ours out. The dehumidifiers keep leather from blooming and paper from curling in a Texas August. Our manager Dana Reyes checks the thermostats every Monday. If your things only need to stay out of the heat, a drive-up unit is cheaper. If they warp, mildew, or crack in humidity, this is the row you want.
