# PAS / PASTOR

Canonical spec. Defined once here; playbooks reference this file, they do not re-teach it.

## What it is
- **PAS** = Problem -> Agitate -> Solve. Name the reader's problem in their own words, sharpen the felt cost of leaving it, then present the service as the resolution.
- **PASTOR** (Ray Edwards) is the extended sales-letter form: Problem -> Amplify -> Story -> Testimony -> Offer -> Response. The Story/Testimony middle is where proof and the operator's real experience live.
- It is a **sequencing spine**, not a page section. It orders the problem-to-proof middle of a page so belief is earned before the ask.

## When to use for local (emergency vs considered)
- **Emergency / high-pain intent** is the home of PAS: burst pipe, AC dead in July, injury, urgent repair. The buyer already feels the pain, so naming it in their words earns the right to pitch fast.
- **Considered / low-pain intent** (remodel, cosmetic dental, estate planning): PAS is weaker because there is no acute pain to agitate. Use AIDA or StoryBrand instead (see `aida-and-4ps.md`, `storybrand-sb7.md`). Copyhackers' own rule: PAS when there is real pain, AIDA when there is not.

## Local adaptation
- **Agitate with concrete operational and financial cost, never manufactured fear.** The leak that becomes a deck replacement, the small cavity that becomes a root canal. Every amplified cost must be substantiable.
- **Amplify locally where a real local condition applies:** hail season, hard-water scale, clay-soil foundations, ice dams. This is coverage the generic AI answer cannot synthesize and a city spoke can inherit and localize.
- The Problem statement is a **citation magnet** for the informational band ("signs you need a new roof"), where AI Overviews show ~92% of the time. Write each symptom as a self-contained, liftable statement.

## PASS test
- The problem is stated in **customer-verbatim language** pulled from VoC (see `voice-of-customer-mining.md`), not the company's phrasing.
- Agitation cites a **real, substantiable cost**, not invented fear.
- The solve names a **mechanism**, not just a service ("most leaks come back because the flashing was never replaced, only the shingles").
- A searcher who typed "signs I need [service]" could paste the problem block as a standalone answer.

## Anti-pattern
- Agitation in the company's words ("your roof may be compromised") instead of the buyer's ("you keep finding little rocks in the gutter").
- Manufactured fear with no cost the business can substantiate (this trips **Law 20**: no fabricated urgency).
- Jumping from hero straight to "why choose us" with no problem articulation, which loses the researcher and the informational traffic.

## Evidence grade
**Craft consensus.** Widely-used default with high explanatory power; the only controlled-lift evidence is DTC and does not transfer to local services. Use it because it sequences belief, then test it. Do not quote a lift figure to a client.
