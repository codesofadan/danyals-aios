# Schwartz - 5 Stages of Awareness x 5 Stages of Market Sophistication

Canonical spec (Eugene Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*). Defined once here; playbooks reference this file.

## What it is
- **Awareness** (how much the buyer already knows): Unaware -> Problem-Aware -> Solution-Aware -> Product-Aware -> Most-Aware. The directness of the copy must match the buyer's stage.
- **Sophistication** (how many competitors have made the same claim in this market): stage 1 = a plain claim works; stage 5 = every claim is worn out and only a mechanism or a specific cuts through.
- This is a **brief-time selector, not a page section.** It decides how direct the copy is and what the lead claim must be, before a word is written.

## When to use for local (emergency vs considered)
Every page, at the `/brief` step. The **target query reveals the awareness stage**, which maps loosely onto pain:
- "emergency plumber near me" -> Solution/Product-Aware -> be direct, lead with the ask and proof (emergency).
- "why is my water bill so high" -> Problem-Aware -> educate first, then pitch (often considered/diagnostic).
- a brand search -> Most-Aware -> just make the ask frictionless.

## Local adaptation
- **Match directness to the query's awareness.** A Product-Aware buyer wants the phone number and one trust signal, not a 300-word education; a Problem-Aware buyer bounces off a hard pitch.
- **Market sophistication decides the lead claim.** In a saturated local market, "24/7 emergency service" is table stakes, not a differentiator. Lead with a **mechanism or a first-hand specific** ("the flashing is why your last repair failed"), which is the system's first-hand-specificity gate restated as a positioning decision.
- Sophistication is read live off the SERP and the local competitor set, not assumed.

## PASS test
- The copy's **directness matches the query's awareness stage** (no education dumped on a ready buyer; no hard ask on a problem-aware researcher).
- The **lead claim is not one every competitor in the SERP already makes**; if it is, it is replaced with a mechanism or a specific.

## Anti-pattern
- One directness setting for every page regardless of query intent.
- Leading a saturated-market page with a table-stakes claim everyone else already makes.
- Educating a Most-Aware brand searcher instead of removing friction from the ask.

## Evidence grade
**Craft consensus**, drawn from a foundational primary text (*Breakthrough Advertising*); no modern controlled study grades it for local pages. Structural and diagnostic. Do not quote a lift figure to a client.
