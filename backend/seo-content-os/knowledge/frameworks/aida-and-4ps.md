# AIDA / 4 Ps

Canonical spec. Defined once here; playbooks reference this file.

## What it is
- **AIDA** = Attention -> Interest -> Desire -> Action. A desire-led sequence: catch attention, build interest, escalate desire for the outcome, then ask.
- **4 Ps** (Promise -> Picture -> Proof -> Push) is the tighter operational variant: promise the outcome, picture it vividly, prove it with specifics, push to one action. Good default skeleton for a service-city money page.
- Both are the low-pain counterpart to PAS: where there is no acute problem to agitate, you lead with the wanted outcome instead.

## When to use for local (emergency vs considered)
- **Considered / elective / desire-led work** is the home of AIDA and the 4 Ps: kitchen remodel, bathroom renovation, cosmetic dentistry, landscaping, estate/financial planning. The buyer is choosing a future they want, not escaping a pain they feel.
- **Emergency / high-pain intent:** do not use AIDA. Use PAS (`pas-and-pastor.md`); the pain is the hook and agitating it is faster than building desire.

## Local adaptation
- **Desire is the picturable outcome:** "a kitchen you actually want to cook in," "a roof that lasts 30 years," "a smile you stop hiding." Make it concrete and sensory, not adjectival.
- **Proof is the local, verifiable specific** the system already mandates (Law 16): real project photos, real named results, real neighborhoods. Desire without proof reads as a brochure.
- **Push is one action.** The 4 Ps end where every local page ends: a single first-person CTA (see `copyhackers-hero-and-belief.md` and `scan-layer-formatting.md`).

## PASS test
- The Desire/Picture block names a **specific, picturable outcome**, not a category ("beautiful new kitchen" fails; "an island big enough that the kids do homework while you cook" passes).
- Proof sits **next to** the desire it supports, not pooled at the bottom (Bencivenga distributed proof; see `value-equation-and-risk-reversal.md`).
- Exactly one action closes the sequence.

## Anti-pattern
- Using AIDA on emergency intent, where desire-building wastes the buyer's urgency.
- "Desire" written as unquantified adjectives ("stunning," "premium," "world-class") with no picturable specific.
- Two co-equal pushes splitting intent at the close.

## Evidence grade
**Craft consensus.** Century-old structural default; no controlled study proves lift over an alternative for local pages. Structural aid only. Do not quote a lift figure to a client.
