# Value Equation (Hormozi) + Risk Reversal

Canonical spec. Defined once here; playbooks reference this file.

## What it is
- **Value equation:** perceived value = (dream outcome x perceived likelihood of achievement) / (time delay x effort & sacrifice). A diagnostic for why a page's offer feels weak.
- **Risk reversal:** a named guarantee that shifts perceived risk from buyer to seller. The highest-leverage under-used local lever. Its whole job is lifting the "perceived likelihood" term and removing a stated objection.

## When to use for local (emergency vs considered)
- **Value equation: every page, as a diagnostic** at the draft-review step. Ask which of the four dials is weakest on this draft.
- **Risk reversal: every page with a real ask,** placed at the point of ask. It matters most on **considered / high-ticket** work (remodel, high-cost dental, legal retainer) where the buyer's perceived risk is highest, and on **emergency** work as an on-time or workmanship guarantee that de-risks a rushed decision.

## Local adaptation
- For local services the two dials the copy actually moves are **perceived likelihood** (proof, guarantee, credential) and **effort/sacrifice** (frictionless CTA; "we handle the permit, the haul-away, the cleanup"). Dream outcome is set by the service; time delay is often fixed. Spend the copy on the two you can move.
- **Risk-reversal catalog** (name the real one from `brand.yaml`, never invent terms): workmanship warranty with years, manufacturer warranty, satisfaction/refund, "no mess left behind," free re-inspection, no-obligation quote, and the punitive on-time guarantee.
- A guarantee that **costs the business money when it fails** (Benjamin Franklin Plumbing's "$5/minute if we're late," up to $300) is a credibility signal a hollow "satisfaction guaranteed" badge can never be.
- Pair the guarantee with the **cost-of-inaction frame** (the leak that becomes a deck replacement) using costs the business can substantiate. This is the legitimate, Law-20-safe form of urgency.

## PASS test
- The draft is scored on **all four value-equation dials**, and the weakest is named and addressed.
- The page states **one specific, real, honorable guarantee with its terms, placed at or near the CTA.**
- Every guarantee term is sourced to `brand.yaml` or the SME, never invented.

## Anti-pattern
- A vague "satisfaction guaranteed" with no terms; a guarantee so hedged in fine print it reads as a trap.
- No risk reversal at all on a high-ticket service (a conversion leak).
- An inaction cost the business cannot substantiate (trips **Law 20**: no fabricated urgency).
- **Quoting a percentage lift for guarantees to a client** (see evidence grade).

## Evidence grade
- **Value equation: craft consensus / diagnostic.** A useful lens, not a measured model. No lift coefficient.
- **Risk reversal: mechanism is sound (folklore on magnitude).** The widely-cited "guarantees lift conversion X%" claims are mostly vendor case studies, not controlled trials. The mechanism (shifting perceived risk from buyer to seller) needs no fabricated coefficient. **Do not quote a percentage lift to a client.**
