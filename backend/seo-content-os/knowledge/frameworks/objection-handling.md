# Objection-Handling (Friction-and-Anxiety Reduction)

Canonical spec. Defined once here; playbooks reference this file. Governs the FAQ block and any inline objection answer.

## What it is
Every unanswered objection is a silent exit. Objection-handling treats the FAQ and inline reassurances not as a help center but as **friction-and-anxiety reduction**: surface the real reasons a buyer hesitates and answer each one before it costs the sale. Each answer doubles as an **AI-citation surface** because it is a self-contained, question-shaped passage.

## When to use for local (emergency vs considered)
- **Every page with a buying decision, both intents.** The FAQ is where it concentrates.
- **Emergency:** front-load the objections that block a fast call (price, "will you actually come now," licensing/insurance).
- **Considered:** the objection set is longer and more deliberative (fit, timeline, warranty, financing, disruption, what happens after I book).

## Local adaptation
- **Order the FAQ the way a buyer raises objections, not the way the business thinks about them:** price -> "will this work for my situation" -> timeline -> warranty -> what happens after I book -> licensing/insurance -> financing -> cleanup/disruption.
- Map questions to the **real "People Also Ask"** and to **what the SME actually gets asked** on the phone, not invented FAQs.
- **Localize where it matters:** permit questions, local warranty terms, service-radius questions.
- Schema note (do not oversell): ship the `FAQPage` node for parsing completeness, but **Google deprecated FAQ rich results as of May 2026.** The active ingredient is the on-page Q&A content, which AI engines lift with or without the markup. Never sell FAQPage as a rich-result or guaranteed AI-citation win.

## PASS test
- The FAQ answers the **real price / fit / timeline / risk objections**, not office-hours trivia.
- **Every answer passes the paste-alone test:** it fully answers its own question with no dependence on a prior paragraph.
- Each answer carries at least one checkable fact and is sourced, never invented.

## Anti-pattern
- No FAQ, or an FAQ that answers only logistics and dodges price, risk, and fit.
- Answers that need prior context to parse (fail the standalone test).
- Marking up FAQPage and telling the client it will produce a rich snippet (false in 2026).

## Evidence grade
- **Objection-handling as a discipline: craft consensus.** Long-standing sales and CRO practice; no single controlled coefficient.
- **The Q&A-as-citation-surface claim is better than consensus:** the Princeton/Georgia Tech GEO study (KDD 2024, single study, directional) found cited, self-contained, statistic-bearing passages lift generative-engine citation, while keyword stuffing reduced it. Treat the direction as supported, the magnitude as directional; re-verify before quoting externally.
